How it works

From bill to receipt in three steps

Built for volunteers and members — minimal friction, clear records.

01 · Admin

Monthly bills are generated

The admin generates a Rs. 50 invoice for every active member at the start of the month.

02 · Member

Pay your preferred way

Use Razorpay card/UPI checkout, pay directly through UPI and submit the UTR, or hand cash to a collector.

03 · System

Receipt & history

Completed payments receive a numbered, printable receipt and appear in the member's payment history.

Payment options

Razorpay
